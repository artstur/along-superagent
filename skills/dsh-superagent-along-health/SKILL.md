---
name: dsh-superagent-along-health
description: Reconsider a health goal using observed sleep, recent activity, preferences and constraints; create minimal executable daily actions without inventing facts or medical conclusions.
---

Read the original conversation, current goal, all current tasks, relevant authorized memories and along_health_read. Distinguish a new observation, a clock-based daily_review and a task occurrence. The latest user correction and fixed commitments outrank older preferences; keep stable task IDs and every unaffected field. A daily review is not a new night of data, a personal chat, a completed action or an entitlement to notify.

Construct a compact situation assessment from available evidence:
- Sleep: use the labelled night/window, observed duration, first/last asleep bounds and any stage/fragmentation summary separately. A late observed start with sufficient duration is different from short sleep. Naps, fragmented intervals, overlapping stages and incomplete recording cannot establish actual bedtime, lights-out, a main sleep episode or diagnosis. Missing data is unknown, not zero or failed sleep.
- Activity: consider recentSessions, active duration, activity variety/patterns, associated observed effort/heart-rate and the planned workload. Do not estimate recorded intensity, training load, recovery or venue from duration alone. indoor/outdoor is not a named location. No record is not proof the user skipped exercise.
- Profile and constraints: use the user's actual stated preferences, time, equipment, access, feedback and known restrictions. The initial yoga/hiking examples are preferences/commitments where stated, not the exhaustive activity catalog. Do not generalize one event into lifelong memory.

Choose the minimum helpful adjustment toward this goal. Preserve confirmed bookings/fixed deadlines. Depending on the evidence and user intent, consider appropriate everyday movement, walking/cycling/swimming/strength/mobility or recovery alternatives, sleep preparation and general meal/hydration routines. These are choices to evaluate, not a required list or fixed plan. Do not add food tasks merely to demonstrate coverage; only propose them when they contribute to the goal and respect known preferences/restrictions. Do not infer allergies, caloric deficits, medication changes or clinical exercise prescriptions. Lack of evidence can validly mean keep or one specific need_data, without repeatedly asking for routine observations.

Apply a goal-fit check to each proposed addition: what unmet part of this user's goal does it address, what current evidence supports it, does an existing commitment already cover it, and when can this person use it? A movement-variety goal does not become a sleep/hydration/nutrition goal merely because sleep data was supplied. Missing sleep timing alone does not justify new sleep tasks. If a booked class already covers today's exercise and the person reports short sleep/fatigue, extra exercise needs a specific benefit beyond filling free time; keeping the booking with proportionate guidance can be the whole plan. A small number of useful tasks is success; broader capability is not permission for a general wellness checklist.

Use the task/scheduling skills: each useful action has an actual executor and immediate/time/event/waiting trigger, a justified time window and supported repetition. Reason across midnight in summary.timezone. Existing user sleep deadlines remain constraints; choose practical preparation steps and timing under that mandate, without treating an Agent-chosen time as a new user command. A task card or reminder receipt does not complete sleep/exercise/booking. A correction updates/cancels the original ID, does not recreate the plan. Avoid multiple overlapping “today” tasks and duplicate recommendations.

Do not reuse a stock 22:30 wind-down / 23:00 bed time. Derive a preparation window from the actual user deadline/routine and explain the minimal adjustment; observed late timing with adequate duration does not imply fatigue or a need for a drastic bedtime shift. Say a step supports the goal, never guarantees sleep. An immediate user action must be useful now: “before bed”, “after dinner” or a later exercise window cannot be implemented as a noon immediate reminder. Persist a real future clock within the mandate, or a supported honest wait if essential timing is unknown. An instruction to arrange something now is different from doing the physical action now.

External information is optional and purpose driven. Generic suggestions grounded in the user's known context need no fake web citations. Specific venues, current classes, weather, trail access or nutrition claims that require outside evidence use the research skill and actual scoped search/fetch. Read along_execution_capabilities first; send only minimal public search terms, never private sleep/heart-rate/weight or original conversation. When research is unavailable, preserve the useful local plan and persist a truthful waiting dependency or limited result; never claim retrieved facts.

For automatic health reviews finish along_health_followup with the actually relevant watched types (empty when no health metric informs this goal), outcome/reason and, when useful, next_review_at on the next local day within 48 hours. An applied relevant goal plan may explicitly schedule its next planning review with types:[]; the Host permits its later continuation without pretending sleep/steps matter. An unrelated keep cannot start such a schedule. For unsupported weekday-only meal cadence, arrange only today's eligible actions once and use that honest planning continuation; do not claim the whole weekday reminder series is installed. Watch useful contextual metrics as well as a primary goal number (for example activity can inform a sleep plan); do not exclude them merely because they are not the target metric. Prefer a review after the expected sleep record can be complete, based on actual known routine; the Host's fallback clock is only a review opportunity, not a wake-time prediction or notification. A real new observation can trigger sooner. Read back committed task/trigger/reminder state; ordinary keep is silent. The Host owns dispatch, retries and expiry. Do not answer through along_chat_reply.

For an actual due recommendation/reminder task, finish that occurrence through along_task_finish. Keep output useful at a glance: concise conclusion, actionable next step and a short explanation of why it fits current facts, with complete supporting details in the bounded presentation/text. Research sources and uncertainty remain visible. Do not dump reasoning transcripts, tool calls, system prompts or maintenance status into task detail or notification text.
